## Platform

web

## Users

Primary audience, confirmed by Minseok:

- **Industry recruiters** screening candidates for ML, computer vision and medical imaging internships and full-time roles. They skim quickly and need to see role fit, current status and contact routes within seconds.
- **Hiring engineers** reviewing technical depth. They open project pages, read methods and results, and check whether the work is real, rigorous and reproducible (papers, numbers, code links).

Academic supervisors and paper readers were not chosen as primary audiences; the site may serve them, but decisions should favor recruiters and engineers.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Minseok Kwak (MSc Artificial Intelligence and Medical Imaging, UCL, Department of Medical Physics and Biomedical Engineering). It exists to get him interviews for ML / medical imaging roles. Success means a recruiter or engineer understands what he works on, sees evidence that it is strong, and contacts him.

## Positioning

Binding differentiators, confirmed by Minseok:

1. **First-author medical imaging paper:** SEAMLESS, a self-supervised, specularity-aware monocular depth and pose method for GI endoscopy, submitted to IPCAI (first author, with Francisco Vasconcelos).
2. **3D vision applied to the clinic:** research that carries 3D perception and depth estimation into surgical and endoscopic imaging, rather than general computer vision alone.

Other true facts (supporting, not lead positioning): third author on SpatialMosaic (arXiv:2512.23365) from the SNU VGI Lab; lead developer on a UCL x National Autistic Society motion-capture game; ROK Army network communications specialist with squad-leader duties.

## Operating Context

- Visitors arrive from a CV, LinkedIn, GitHub or an application link, usually on desktop during screening, sometimes on a phone.
- Each project has its own detail page (SEAMLESS, SpatialMosaic, SHADeS++, SuperHero SportsDay) that engineers read for method and results.
- Contact happens by email, LinkedIn, GitHub or phone, all listed on the site.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site hosted on GitHub Pages (repository `mkwak13/mkwak13.github.io`), plain HTML, CSS and JS per page.
- **Copy rule (binding):** no em dashes in site copy.
- Paper status must be stated accurately: SEAMLESS is an IPCAI submission; SpatialMosaic is an arXiv preprint. Do not upgrade either to "accepted" without confirmation.
- Undecided: whether the SEAMLESS PDF should be publicly linked while under review (depends on IPCAI's review policy).

## Product Principles

1. Lead with the medical imaging research; everything else supports it.
2. Show the work itself (figures, depth maps, results) before describing it.
3. Every claim is checkable: real numbers, real paper links, accurate status.
4. A recruiter gets role fit and a contact route in the first screen; an engineer gets method and results one click away.
